The ~2,000 most frequent English words are baked into this font. Rarer words render unchanged here (the rule-based fallback isn't in the font yet — that's the next build). Works in Chrome / Firefox / Safari (HarfBuzz & CoreText); not in Windows DirectWrite apps. See the README.

The font is silent by design — it changes how text looks, not sound. The 🔊 button is your browser's built-in speech voice (Web Speech API) reading the original English; it is not part of the font.
